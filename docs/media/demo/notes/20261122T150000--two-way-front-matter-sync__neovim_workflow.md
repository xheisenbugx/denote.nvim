Edit your title or tags directly in YAML, TOML, or Org front matter, then
run `:Denote rename_from_front_matter` (`<leader>nR`) to update the filename
on disk instantly.

Links to [Plain-text note taking with Denote](denote:20261118T091500) remain intact.
